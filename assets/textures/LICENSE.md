# Detail texture library: licenses

Every texture in this folder comes from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)
and is released under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain
dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/; see
https://ambientcg.com/license). No attribution is required; it is given here
anyway.

The 1K-JPG downloads were reduced (box filter, so tiling stays seamless) and
recompressed: `color.jpg` and `normal.jpg` (OpenGL convention) at 1024 px,
`roughness.jpg`, `height.jpg` (displacement) and `ao.jpg` (when the asset
has one) at 512 px.
